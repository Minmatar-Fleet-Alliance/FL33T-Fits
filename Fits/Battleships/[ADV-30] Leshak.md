# Leshak

Close range, high EHP faction battleship

## Description

High DPS, brick tanked armor battleship. Weak to ECM, energy neutralizers, and anything else that can break its spooled damage. Mid-grade amulets are a soft requirement for usage of these in low security space, and are recommended in null security space with the appropriate escape bay frigate.
